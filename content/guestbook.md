# Guestbook

If you want to sign the guestbook, send an email to
[admin@brightprogrammer.in](mailto:admin@brightprogrammer.in?subject=Guestbook%20entry).

Include:

- the name you want shown
- your website, if you have one
- the short message you want listed

Use a real email address. Disposable mail domains may be ignored.
