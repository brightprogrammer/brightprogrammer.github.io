# Expressiveness Of Thoughts

Long time ago, in a book on concepts of programming languages, I read :

> A man can think better if he as better language to express himself!  

Better here means, being able to express complex thoughts precisely (not necessarily easily, not hard either). Now I find it to be fundamentally true (because I'm experiencing the fact in my life) and it's now really important to start gaining meanings of exact words, so that I'm able to express myself better (atleast in my own thoughts).

To exactly quote [the book](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/603257.Concepts_of_Programming_Languages) by Sebesta :

> **Increased capacity to express ideas**. It is widely believed that the depth at which people can think is influenced by the expressive power of the language in which they communicate their thoughts. Those with only a weak understanding of natural language are limited in the complexity of their thoughts, particularly in depth of abstraction. In other words, it is difficult for people to conceptualize structures they cannot describe, verbally or in writing.

And that is exactly what is happening with me here.

# Who Am I?

Or What am I? As a starting point, I'll define myself as

> **An observer who can observe itself and a part of universe who thinks it has the ability to participate in causality of affect chain of events**.

"I" have the following properties:

- "I" can observe others (YOU)
- "I" uses "I" to refer to itself!
- "I" can observe itself ("I")!
- "I" can take actions based on it's observations
- "I" has an "attribute" called "Memory" that can keep track of it's observations
- "I" has "sense" of time, maybe as a consequence of having "Memory"
- "I" has the "ability" to take actions based on it's observations present in it's "Memory"
- "I" has a unique "Memory" that differentiate it from others (YOU)

But all these points only define the properties of "I", so who exactly is "I"? or am "I"? At the moment, any thing, object, part of universe that can satisfy these properties, can use the term "I" for itself! Since I satisfy all these properties, I'll answer that I'm a part of this universe that satisfies these properties whenever this question will come in my mind. That's the definition I wanna begin with, even if it's wrong, atleast I have something to answer to whenver this question crosses my mind.

Well now we have a definition that we can use to answer other questions, great! Note that at this point, this definition is completely my own, (alteast that's what my memory tells me now). I'd like to improve and redefine it in future once I start learning from books written by other philosophers.

# Do I Exist?

Now that I have a definition of myself, I'll define "What do I mean by existence?". For the moment, let's say, anything that can participate in causality with or without it's own consent, i.e, either by itself or by getting controlled by others, is in existence, i.e, it exists. 
By the current definition of "I", I have a very high probability of existing! Because, from the second last point, I can take actions. If I can take actions that can act as cause and make some effects then yes I do exist by current definition of existence. So, now I just have to prove that my actions affect this universe, i.e bring a change in it's state. By state, I mean arrangement/position of parts of it.

It's fairly easy to prove that my actions really do change this universe's state! We start by answering the question what do we mean by change of state. I'll define it as being different from previous state, i.e. not equal. I'll take slight mathematical approach here to answer this :

- I define a set that contains ALL possible state that an object/entity can be in!
- If this set is singleton, i.e contains only one element, then it's PURE, otherwise it's IMPURE!

Now the following implications arise : 

- Sincce set has a property that all elements in the set are different from each other,
- Perfoming any set/sequence of operations on a PURE cannot change it's state
- Perfoming any set/sequence of operations on an IMPURE may or may not change it's state.
- Any object with more than one possible state is IMPURE.
- We can say, this universe is an IMPURE.

Hence if I perform a finite number of operations/actions in this universe, I'll definitely change it's state because if it takes infinite steps to change it's state then it's PURE by definition.

Therefore, yes I do exist based on current defintions I provide
